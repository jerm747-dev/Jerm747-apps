747 GP wheel references

Each class has one front and one rear tire-and-rim design. Use each design on both sides of its axle. Front and rear cutouts within a class use the same scale. Each PNG has a transparent 640 × 640 canvas. Straight-on views center the outer tire outline on the canvas. In the angled views, the outer face is on the left and the tire extends to the right.

Wheel-dimensions-mm.csv gives the outside tire diameter and tire width from the uploaded game prototype. Use these numbers to size a 3D mesh. The images supply visual references for profile, spoke shape, tread and finish. Calipers and brake discs are separate from the wheel.

The kart and Formula references use the slick tread from the approved vehicle artwork; the coupe and supercar use road tread.
