How does this compare to Blender?

This works like Blender's Decimate modifier (Collapse mode): it removes triangles but keeps your UVs and textures. Blender's Remesh works differently: it rebuilds the surface as new geometry and throws away UVs.

Counts here are triangles. To compare in Blender, turn on Statistics in the viewport overlay and look at "Triangles". Meshy's "polycount" for quad models is roughly half the triangle count.
