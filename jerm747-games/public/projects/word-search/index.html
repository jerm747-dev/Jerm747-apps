    How to play

    Drag a straight line across a word, or tap its first and last letters. Words run in eight directions, forward or backward.

    Keyboard: arrow keys move; Enter or Space selects the first and last letters. Escape cancels a selection. Hint reveals the start of a remaining word.

    New puzzle lets you change topic, choose 15, 30 or 60 words, and keep, randomize or pick the word set. Restart clears found words on the same board. Progress saves on this device.

    Expand puzzle gives the board more room. Use Scroll mode if you need to pan.

    Find a new groove

    Choose a topic and challenge, or keep your words and shuffle the grid.
