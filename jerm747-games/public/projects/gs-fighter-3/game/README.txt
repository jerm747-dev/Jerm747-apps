PLAY IN A BROWSER
Extract the whole ZIP, keep the folder together, and open GS-Fighter-III/index.html
in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (hardware acceleration on). Works from the folder; no
internet needed. To play on a phone, host the folder on any web server (for example
jerm747.games) and open it there.

FLOW
Intro → (leave it 20 s for the CPU demo) → PRESS START → Fighter Select →
best-of-three match vs the CPU → results: Rematch / Fighter Select / Title.

FIGHTER SELECT
Hold a fighter. P1/P2 appear beside your finger; slide left/right and release
to assign. A short vibration marks the touch, popup, each change, and selection.
Hold/release without moving to leave the popup open, then tap or slide between
the circles. Drag outside the choice row and release to cancel.
Keyboard: arrows, Enter. The Vibration option controls these pulses too.
Controller: left stick P1, right stick P2, LB/RB lock, Start begins.
14 fighters: Akeem, Nika and the twelve newcomers. Newcomers load the first time
they are picked and share the Akeem/Nika move set from their starter plan.

GAME      Resume · Restart match · Fighter select · Quit to title

KEYBOARD (Arrows layout)
← →  move (double-tap → and hold to run; tap ← to step back)
↑ jump (with → or ← for forward/back) · ↓ or C crouch · Space backflip
L / J / U  low / mid / high punch      K / O / P  low / mid / high kick
B block · V V-Wave · crouch + K sweep · in the air: kick = flying kick, punch = air punch

CONTROLLER (standard layout)
D-pad / left stick move · LB low punch · X mid punch · Y high punch
LT low kick · A mid kick · B high kick · RB block · RT V-Wave · right stick camera

Phones: slide your thumb around the round pad; ↗ jumps forward, ↖ backflips.

ARTWORK UPDATE 1.01A
12 new hand-painted fighter-select portraits and matching face tiles, following
the existing Akeem/Nika style. Original Akeem and Nika art is preserved.
Brush spirals and each fighter's V-Wave share one energy color definition.
Controllers, moves, match rules, models and stage assets are unchanged.

TOUCH SLIDE UPDATE 1.01B
Only the P1/P2 popup and its touch behavior have changed. The approved fighter
selection screen, portraits, roster, controller mappings and gameplay remain.
The touch popup follows the original touch location and stays inside the screen.
Each change gives one short tick; keeping the finger still does not keep buzzing.
The chooser retains the 360 ms hold so you can still scroll the fighter roster.
The keyboard/controller popup remains centered and keyboard accessible.
For a quick touch-only preview, open touch-select-preview.html.
Vibration requires a compatible device/browser and the game's Vibration setting.
Physical vibration strength should be checked on your Android device.
