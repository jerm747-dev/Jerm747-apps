How to play

Controls

Move with the stick, WASD or arrows. A / Space jumps, up to three times in the air. B / Shift dashes, in the air too. X / J slashes and Y / K swings heavy; tap in rhythm to chain. L / Q switches walk and run. R / E climbs trees, rocks, lanterns and gates, and pulls the sword at the summit. Start / Esc opens the menu; Select / Tab opens the Silver Shrine.

Stone steps rise off the path: jump up them to open a shrine cache (+10 silver). In the tunnels the light drops away and Shadow becomes a silhouette; follow the lanterns.

Cut brush and trees for silver, or flush out rabbits and lizards and run them down. Silver is saved: spend it at the Silver Shrine.
