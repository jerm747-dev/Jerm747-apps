New image-generated artwork applied to Fighter Select:
- akeem-body-brush.png: transparent full-body guard pose, 1024x1536.
- nika-body-brush.png: transparent full-body guard pose, 1024x1536.
- akeem-head-brush.png: separate transparent headshot, 1254x1254.
- nika-head-brush.png: separate transparent headshot, 1254x1254.
- spiral-cyan.png: transparent broad brush spiral behind Akeem.
- spiral-gold.png: transparent broad brush spiral behind Nika.

The original fighting poses and costumes, snapshots rendered from the current
3D game rigs, and the GS Fighter III title logo supplied the references.
The logo guides brushwork only; the portraits contain no title lettering.
These are new dimensional paintings, not literal captures of the 3D models.
The spiral assets are separate layers so future fighters can use their own
approved blast colors. PNG pixels and transparency are kept as generated;
positioning, mirroring, scale and soft edge blending happen in the select UI.

Original uploaded sheets retained unchanged in the game build:
akeem-original.png — source 42884.png, 1536x1024.
nika-original.png — source 42885.png, 1536x1024.

The standalone Fighter Artwork ZIP contains the six finished new PNGs and
these source/prompt notes. It excludes discarded drafts and original meshes.
Preserve PNG alpha when importing or reusing the artwork.
BRUSH-ART-PROMPTS.txt records the prompts for extending this style.
