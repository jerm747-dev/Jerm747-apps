spiral-cyan
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG background decoration behind a full-body fighter on the GS Fighter III select screen. Use the supplied logo ONLY as the reference for thick, energetic directional dry-brush paint strokes, ragged bristle edges and layered paint thickness. Generate one large circular SPIRAL of broad sweeping brush strokes: an open swirling V-Wave energy vortex, roughly one and a half turns, an irregular broken outer circle and a curl winding inward. It is painted brushwork rather than literal fire, smoke, glossy sci-fi rings or vector line art. Colors: electric cyan and deep teal, icy pale highlights, a few restrained indigo shadows. Rich dimensional pigment, alternating broad heavy strokes and fine bristle trails. The center has ample transparent space so a fighter's face and torso can sit in front without clutter. Single isolated spiral, no character, no text or symbols, no particles filling the whole canvas. Square composition, whole spiral fits with 8% transparent margin. Genuine transparent background, including gaps between strokes and an empty center. Bold legible brush silhouette when used subtly behind a fighter.
