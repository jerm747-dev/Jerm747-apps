GS FIGHTER III — TEXTURE LAB v0.30
Built for Jeremy / Jerm747 — October 31, 2026

START
Extract the complete ZIP and open fighter-lab/index.html, or host the folder
on your usual web server. The embedded assets remain included for offline use.
No new CDN or runtime dependency was added. Android file-manager access may
require a browser-accessible local/server URL, as in previous builds.

NEW INNER FLOOR
A 160-segment disk of radius 11.65 m lies inside the original stone ring.
It shares the ring's boundary height and sits 1 mm above the existing terrace,
which continues underneath and outside it. The ring geometry is unchanged.
The inner floor has its own color texture and packed surface map. Its default
slabs are mapped at 3x the original outer-stone size, with a separate UV offset
to keep a reflected tile corner away from the middle of the fighting lane.
All stone coordinates remain in world space during camera motion.

Settings > Stone & lighting > Inner slab size: 1.5x–5x, default 3.00x.
Outer stone scale still adjusts the original outer/ring/wall material only.
Rotation, moisture, key light and relief retain their existing shared controls.
Foreground shade applies to both floor materials and the ridge; fighters and
walls remain outside that shading pass. It fades from full strength at 1.45x
zoom to zero at 3.2x, with a default maximum darkening of 42%.

MOUNTAIN JOINS
One native-resolution asymmetric scenic insert is shared by four curved bands,
centered over the reflected panorama boundaries. Each is 40% of one scenic
panel's width. Its side edges feather to zero; the shore and upper sky also
fade into the approved panorama. Alternating orientation and slight sampling
offsets vary the placement. A 2 cm radius inset keeps the inserts clear of the
underlying panel surface. They follow the same aspect-dependent geometry fit.
The original scenic panels and sky asset remain unchanged. Water glints are
masked to the lower blue river pixels. The underlying wings still repeat;
these are local seam transitions, not a new continuous 360-degree painting.

QUALITY SELECTION
Settings > Texture quality: 2K / 4K Ultra / 8K Extreme. Fresh launches request
8K, subject to GPU texture-size support. The selection is remembered; changing
it reloads and resets the lab. The hardware texture limit is not a guarantee
of enough GPU memory or sustained frame rate. If 8K is heavy on your device,
choose 4K or 2K. Recovery links:
  index.html?quality=balanced&skipintro=1
  index.html?quality=ultra&skipintro=1
  index.html?quality=extreme&skipintro=1

RESOLUTION / PROVENANCE
- Original outer-stone generated source: 1254 x 1254, retained unchanged.
- New v0.30 inner-slab generated source: 1254 x 1254.
- Approved v0.29 sky panorama source: 1402 x 1122, retained unchanged.
- New mountain-join generated source: 887 x 1774, used at native resolution.
All artwork was made with built-in image generation. Requested 8K output was
not returned at native 8K. The 2K/4K/8K floor/panorama exports use Lanczos3
resampling. They are not AI super-resolution or recovered native 8K detail.
Each stone material has a 2048 x 2048 linear packed map: red = estimated
height, green = roughness, blue = cavity. These are derived shading estimates,
not measured physical scans. Procedural grain supplies additional fine detail.

Mode        Inner / outer floor     Panorama       Color textures*
2K          2048 x 2048 each        2048 x 1638     about 60 MiB
4K Ultra    4096 x 4096 each        4096 x 3276     about 239 MiB
8K Extreme  8192 x 8192 each        8192 x 6552     about 956 MiB

* Approximate uncompressed RGBA8 GPU storage including mipmaps for the three
  large color maps. Also allow about 8 MiB for the shared scenic join and
  43 MiB for the two surface maps, plus shadows, environment lighting, fighters,
  framebuffers, decoded CPU images and browser overhead. WebP reduces file
  size, not the uncompressed GPU allocation. Each scenic map is shared by
  its copies, rather than uploaded separately for every panel.

VALIDATION / LIMITS
Embedded 2K loading and decoding, independent material mapping, disk boundary,
unchanged ring geometry, retained camera coverage, script syntax and all image
references passed. Five material shader variants compiled and linked with
Mesa GLES. The actual panorama and join shaders were rendered to a flat
comparison preview and inspected. This is not a full mobile rendering or
performance test; the existing preview browser cannot create a WebGL context.
The application still uses Three.js r128. Stone relief affects shading, not
collision or silhouette. The backdrop remains painted curved scenery.
