GS FIGHTER III — FIGHTER SELECT — v0.36

OPEN
Extract the whole ZIP and open fighter-lab/index.html. Press Start opens
Fighter Select. During the workshop, Fighter select reopens this menu.
Enter Fighter Lab launches the selected matchup in the existing training lab.
Opening selection clears active projectiles and finish timing. The menu chooses
fighter slots; it does not add human P2 combat controls.

TOUCH / MOUSE
Tap a roster tile: assign that fighter to P1 immediately.
Hold for about 0.32 seconds: two small P1/P2 circles appear just above the tile.
Keep holding, slide a little right toward P2, and release to assign P2.
Hold and release in place: assign P1. Slide back left before release for P1.
The choice stays at the tile; there is no large dialog or dimmed backdrop.
Release far outside to cancel. Moving before the hold starts permits scrolling.
The P1 / P2 shortcut opens the same tiny chooser for the highlighted fighter;
tap either circle to assign. Tap outside or press Escape to dismiss it.
Replace either slot at any time; both slots may use the same fighter.
Tap a large fighter name to browse from that slot's current fighter.
Back unlocks P2, then P1, then returns to the title. Title returns immediately.

KEYBOARD
Arrows or W/A/S/D browse without changing confirmed assignments.
Enter or Space opens the tiny player chooser for the highlighted fighter.
Left/Right chooses P1/P2; Enter or Space confirms. Escape or Backspace cancels
without losing assigned fighters. Tab leaves the chooser and continues through
the menu buttons. Outside the chooser, Escape/Backspace follows Back.
Activate Enter Fighter Lab after both players are ready. Gameplay input is
isolated from the menu; held confirmations do not repeat.

STANDARD GAMEPADS
Left stick browses P1; right stick browses P2 independently.
D-pad browses the current selection step. A / bottom face button selects the
current side, or begins when both are ready. LB or left-stick click locks P1;
RB or right-stick click locks P2. Start begins when both are locked.
B or Back/Select goes back. Release inputs held when opening the menu first.
A second standard controller uses its left stick, D-pad and A for P2.
Within the tiny chooser: sticks choose a circle, A confirms, LB/RB assigns
P1/P2, and B cancels. Controller 2's A assigns P2.
These are browser-standard button positions; printed labels can differ.

ARTWORK AND AURAS
fighter-artwork contains new transparent full-body paintings and separate
headshots for Akeem/Nika. References were the original guard poses, actual
game meshes and the GS Fighter III title's broad brushwork. These are new
dimensional illustrations, not screenshots or a replacement for combat rigs.
Hands, hair and feet fit the preview. P2 faces inward through CSS mirroring.
Separate painted spirals sit behind each figure, with an open center and soft
edge fade: Akeem cyan/teal; Nika gold/amber. The art remains static.
Selecting P1 sets the corresponding V-Wave aura preset: cyan or solar.
The charge, flame shell, wisps, glow and impact use that preset. The supplied
Azure blast core retains its original blue texture. Settings can override the
aura color; resetting the arena preserves that override. Changing the matchup
restores the selected fighter's preset.
Original source sheets are retained unchanged in the same artwork folder.
See SOURCE.txt and BRUSH-ART-PROMPTS.txt there for provenance and prompts.

LAYOUT
Wide and compact landscape layouts place the roster between the full poses.
Portrait puts the roster below them. Touch buttons hide automatically when a
controller connects; Touch buttons: On/Off overrides this for the menu.
Roster tiles scroll when needed; keyboard/gamepad focus stays in view.
Reduced motion disables the brief hold-progress animation.

ADDING A FIGHTER AFTER RIGGING
See NEW-FIGHTER-IMPORTS.txt and NEW-FIGHTER-MOVE-SETS.json first.
After validating and retargeting a skinned rig, load its mesh/clip data before
fighter-roster.js, add FighterDefaults and register a unique id, name, accent
and data() entry. portrait and headshot metadata each use src, width, height
and an SVG viewBox ('x y width height'). Optional clip polygons can isolate
sheet content. Standalone transparent PNGs need no clip polygon. Add spiral
and wavePalette fields for an approved background and aura preset.
The registry drives tiles, independent cursors and mirror matches. A raw FBX
without a skeleton and skin weights is not a playable rig.
