Wave Run 747

Story · Pelican Bay · Level 2 · Before the first heat

Riptide takes off his cap and sets it beside his mango-pineapple smoothie. He’s celebrating making it to Pelican Bay. The smoothie bar is where all the riders come before a heat, so both teams have a view of the counter from the dock.

Across the bar, Mara catches him looking. She smiles. A supporter in her team colors gives him a longer look. Mara carries her guava-pineapple smoothie over, takes the seat beside him, and slips a napkin under her own glass. Then she picks up his cap, puts it back on his head, and straightens the brim. He holds still for her.

Mara “You always gonna take that red buoy that wide?”

Riptide “That’s my cut. I already know when the other riders’ wakes are gonna cross. Soon as they do, I’m cutting in.”

Mara “So that’s what you’re waiting for.”

Riptide “You been watching me that close?”

Mara “Close enough to know where you’ll be.”

The bartender leans in with a pineapple wedge still in his tongs.

Bartender “See that water getting shiny off the point? Wind’s wrapping around it. Gonna be a clean pocket inside that buoy.”

They both turn to look at him. He drops the pineapple onto a glass and goes back to work like somebody asked him.

Riptide “You gonna have to turn pretty sharp to get in there.”

Mara “I already backed my trim down a click. I can get the nose around without scrubbing as much speed.”

She takes a sip.

Mara “You can keep your cut. I only need half a ski.”

Riptide looks back out at the buoy. He can see it now. He opens his mouth, and Mara’s already smiling.

Chief mechanic, from the dock “Skim! Finish that mango. It’s time to tango. Let’s go, let’s go!”

Mara “It’s guava.”

Chief mechanic “Guava’s got two test laps.”

Mara takes one last sip. She taps the brim of Riptide’s cap and leans in, her drawl coming out as she smiles.

Mara “You can’t rip the water, baby. You gotta skim it.”

By the time he thinks of a comeback, she’s halfway down the dock. Riptide sits there with his mango, staring at the red buoy. She got him.

Season outline

Pelican Bay. Mara “Skim” Bennett is a sweetheart with a racer’s eye for wind, wakes, and how her ski responds to trim. Riptide trusts his practice, his cut, and his ability to hold his line in rough water. In the race, Mara finds the pocket and gets half a ski ahead. Riptide carries his speed through the chop and draws level on the straight. The player decides who wins.

Opposing teams. Riptide’s coach pushes him to make Mara race his line. Mara’s chief mechanic wants her test laps and wants their setup kept inside the team. Her team owner sees a rivalry he can sell. Supporters repeat her dockside jabs until every friendly exchange risks turning into a story about the two teams.

Early season. They trade advice on a dawn practice run. He shows her how he sets up a tight exit. She teaches him to read water farther ahead. They start saving each other a spot at the dock.

Midseason. After a rough race in front of her family, Mara admits how badly she wanted that win. Riptide starts diagnosing the turn that cost her, catches himself, and asks if she wants company. On the walk back, he tells her why he studies footage even after he wins. Later, when she checks on him after a bad finish, a supporter’s clip makes it look like she came over to gloat. The clip gets to him before her words do.

Finale. They race each other flat out. After the podium, Riptide asks Mara to stay another day. She asks what he has planned. “I figured we could see where we end up.” She takes his hand. Tomorrow they can ride with no buoys and no clock.

Mara “Skim” Bennett
