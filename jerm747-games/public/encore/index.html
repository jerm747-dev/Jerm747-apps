    ENCORE  /  01

    Find your rhythm.

    A quiet block to explore. Take the sidewalk, hop onto the crates, or climb the awnings and balconies. The camera follows you upward.

    Controller
    Stick or D-pad to move. A jumps, B or X flips, Y switches running on or off. Hold a bumper to run. Right trigger throws, left trigger talks, and right stick click toggles the flashlight. Start pauses. Select cycles touch controls.
    Move
    ← → or A / D
    Jump
    Space, ↑ or W. Release early for a shorter hop. Run first to carry momentum into a long jump. Press Jump again in the air for one upward boost; steer with the direction pad. Landing restores the double jump. There is no third jump.
    Flip
    X. Step into a forward flip from the ground, or tuck during a regular jump. Tap Flip again during a flip or double jump to dive toward the ground, carrying your sideways momentum. Landing restores the move.
    Ground pound
    Hold Down and tap Flip (↓ + X), or tap Down during a flip. Nige tucks into a spin and drops straight down. Land on a gold supply box or a wooden NH crate to open it.
    Speed
    Hold Shift, double-tap a direction, or tap Speed to switch running on/off. For an extra gear, double-tap a direction, then tap Speed (or hold Shift): blue streaks mark the boost. Your jump keeps that momentum. Tap Speed again to switch it off. Reverse while running to brake and slide into the turn.
    Fireballs
    Head-bump a gold crate, then catch the bouncing fireball for three throws. Tap Fire or press F for an ear-level throw with a downward release. Shots bounce farther, then burn out offscreen or fizzle after five seconds. Fireballs also open gold crates. Shoot or ground pound a wooden NH crate, then touch the gear it drops. Fireballs pass through people.
    Next block
    Walk right through the café and Sunset Market. Climb Roofline Rise to its high right-hand exit, then cross Rooftop Run and follow the music to the listening party. The lower street in area three does not lead out. Left exits return to the previous area. Your fireballs, merch and opened crates carry over for this run.
    Duck / drop
    ↓ or S. Press once to crouch. Double-tap within a short window to drop through steps, awnings, balconies and fire escapes—even while rising from a crouch. You land on the next surface below. Building rooftops, gold crates and NH loot crates stay solid.
    After Party
    Choose After Party from the mode menu or your chapter-end collection. Tap Flashlight or press L to switch your beam. Keep an eye out for NH crates. The city might still have a surprise for you on your next run.
    Timed replay
    Choose Time Trial to race from the café to the rooftop party. Your clock stops when you reach the party; pauses, fades and conversations don’t count. Beat your fastest time on this device.
    Pause
    P or Escape

    A speech bubble marks someone you can chat with. Run straight past, or choose Tap to talk / E when nearby. Talking is always optional. Choose Keep walking to leave and return later; finish the chat for one merchant token.

    Uses phone vibration or controller rumble when available.

    Touch: slide around the eight-way D-pad and use Jump or Flip with your other thumb. Upper corners jump and move together.
