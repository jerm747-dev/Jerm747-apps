## MIDI setup

Plug in a USB MIDI device (USB OTG on a phone), then Connect. Needs Chrome or Edge.

Each track sends on its own channel (set it in the track list). Tick Drum to put a track on channel 10. Turn off a track's Out switch to keep it on the built-in sound only.
