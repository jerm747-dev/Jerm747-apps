Hold Down and tap Flip (keyboard Down or S + X). On the ground Nige springs
into a tucked spin. In the air he tucks and drops straight down. You can also
tap Down during a flip. The impact kicks up dust and an expanding ring.
Tap Flip again on its own during a flip or double jump for the forward dive.

Encore, look for the black Night Drop crate on the awning. At the Night
Market, climb the west fire escape. A direct ground pound or a fireball
opens either crate. Touch the black NH tour tee and black NH snapback to
collect them. Both carry white lettering.

tour backpack have their own slots. The two black editions are separate
rewards. The rooftop café shows all six finds with your Gold Mic.
