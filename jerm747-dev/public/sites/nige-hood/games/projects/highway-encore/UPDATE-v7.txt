ENCORE — SUNSET MARKET + FIREBALLS (v7)
3 November 2026

Walk off the café block on the right to fade into a new sunset rooftop market.
The market adds 22 platforms and eight gold reward crates. Walk right again
to loop to the café, or use the market’s left edge to return.

Head-bump a gold crate to release a bouncing fireball. Catch it for three
throws. Press F or tap the new Fire button. Thrown fireballs bounce along
surfaces and can open other gold crates. Pedestrians are unaffected.

Fireball inventory, collected merchant tokens, opened crates and uncollected
fireballs carry between the two blocks during a run. New run resets them.
Progress is not saved after closing or reloading the page.

Walking, running, skidding, jumping and flipping retain the v6 movement.
The existing three action buttons and D-pad keep their positions. Fire sits
in the open space above Speed and has a minimum 44px touch target.

For a quick look at the new area, open index.html?autostart=1&area=market
from the same web server. The normal start still begins at the café.

Validation: 26 engine regression checks, browser play-through of crate pop,
collection, throwing and fade transition; 390×844 portrait and 844×390
landscape control layouts. Physical Android hardware was not available.

Run the regression suite with: node tests/street-game.test.cjs
