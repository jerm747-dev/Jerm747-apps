ENCORE v13 — BACKSTAGE ALLEY

The After Party now opens in a long alley behind Club Encore. Walk right
to reach the amber streetlight, or take the optional fire escape overhead.

Nige starts with his flashlight on. Tap the Flashlight action button or
press L to put it away or bring it back, including while moving. Street
lighting stays on when the flashlight is off.

Head-bump the alley's gold supply box and catch the fireball for three
throws. If you miss every shot, head-bump the box again for another supply.
Jump up the five fire-escape landings. On the top platform, face the wooden
NH loot crate, tap Fire or press F, then touch the backpack it drops.
You can return from Club Encore if you walked past it.

The tour backpack appears as the fourth NH find in the night HUD and in
the rooftop café collection beside your other finds and Gold Mic.
