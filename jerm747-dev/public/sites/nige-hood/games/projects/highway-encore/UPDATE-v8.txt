ENCORE — NH LOOT + DOUBLE JUMP (v8)
3 November 2026

THREE MERCH PRIZES
Shoot the brown wooden NH crates. Each breaks with one fireball, throwing
wood splinters and releasing its item. Touch the item to collect it:
- White T-shirt with black NH: street height in the Café Block.
- White baseball cap with black NH: above the Sunset Market awning.
- Special-edition LP, record and signed-style sleeve: highest Roofline Rise roof.
Crates gently bob and remain solid until destroyed. The collection tracker
fills in as you find each piece; all three trigger a brief FULL SET banner.
Fireballs still bounce and never hurt or interrupt pedestrians.

DOUBLE JUMP
Jump or Flip from the ground, then tap Jump once more in the air for an
upward boost. The boost softens sideways momentum; direction controls let
you steer. Three new sprites show the rise, peak and fall. Landing restores
the air jump. A third press cannot add another midair boost. New spring,
tuck-entry and unfurl poses also smooth the first front flip.

THE ROUTE
Café Block -> Sunset Market -> Roofline Rise -> Rooftop Run.
Area three exits only from its highest roof at the right. Keep travelling
across the buildings in area four; the right edge continues the rooftop loop.
Left exits lead back. Missing a roof safely returns you to your last ledge.
Ammo, collected merch and broken/opened crates carry between areas during
the run. They reset on New run or page reload.

OPTIONAL CHAT
Run straight past the speech bubble without stopping. Tap 'Tap to talk'
or press E when nearby to choose a conversation. 'Keep walking' leaves
without a reward and lets you return later. A completed conversation still
awards one merchant token, once per run.

LIGHT HAPTICS
Fire: short tap. Loot crate destruction: brief two-part rumble.
Uses phone vibration / connected controller haptics when supported.
Turn it off in How to play. The choice is remembered on that browser.
Physical phone/controller rumble has not been feel-tested here.

CONTROLS
Arrows or A/D: move. Space / Up / W: jump, then double jump.
Shift / double-tap direction / Speed: run. X: front flip. F: fire.
E: optional talk. Down / S: duck. P / Escape: pause.

optional talk, one air jump only, real jumps up the third-area climb, high
exit gating, rooftop returns and pause/reset behavior. Haptics checks cover
patterns, opt-out, preference persistence and unavailable/rejected APIs.
Browser QA checks crate destruction and pickup, optional chat, aerial poses,
new environments and portrait/landscape touch layouts.
