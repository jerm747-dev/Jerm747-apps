ENCORE — THROW, BOOST & DIVE (v9)
3 November 2026

FIREBALL THROW
Four new poses: cock beside the ear, drive forward, release slightly down,
then follow through. The projectile, sound and light vibration begin at the
release frame (0.14 seconds). Movement stays responsive during the throw.
A flip or dive interrupts an unreleased throw without spending ammunition.
Shots move at 540 px/sec, with a longer bounce (about 440 px on level ground).
They fizzle into a small smoke-and-ember puff after five active seconds or
when fully offscreen. They cannot hit anything after they have burned out.
Pause freezes their lifetime. Collectible fireball pickups remain available.

EXTRA RUNNING GEAR
Double-tap Left or Right and keep moving to run. While that run is latched,
tap Speed on the controller (or hold Shift) for a faster 416 px/sec boost;
normal running remains 304 px/sec and walking remains 172 px/sec.
Three subtle blue streaks show the extra speed. Tap Speed again to disable
it. Jump and Flip preserve launch speed, with a gentle loss of speed in the
air when Speed is released. Direction input still steers. The second jump
retains more sideways momentum when launched from the extra gear, while
still providing its single upward boost. Reversing direction keeps the skid.

AERIAL DIVE
Tap Flip / X again during a flip or double jump to turn into a downward
dive. Two new sprites show entry and extended descent. Sideways momentum
continues with a small forward burst; steering remains available. The move
lands on platforms with the existing compression/recoil and a light puff.
One dive per airtime; repeated taps cannot stack boosts. Jump does not cancel
a committed dive. Landing or a safe rooftop recovery restores the move.

SQUAT AND ROUTE CLEANUP
Four new grounded poses: shallow bend, deeper bend, hold, then rise.
The head cannot grow through a solid crate when standing up under it.
Removed three crowded gold boxes and moved five others clear of platform
headroom. All gold pickups leave at least full standing clearance on nearby
walking surfaces. The three breakable NH merch crates remain in place.

PRESERVED
Four areas, rooftop loop, double jump, optional conversation, collectible
NH shirt/cap/vinyl, light haptics, controller layout and safe pedestrians.
Motion Workshop shows the new moves at the top; Animate moves loops them.

VALIDATION
57 engine checks pass, including boost/air momentum, dive/landing, throw
release timing, pause, five-second expiry, offscreen culling before hits,
squat headroom and real jumps through the third-area climb. Haptics suite
passes. Browser checks cover the new poses and effects in the actual game.
Physical controller/phone vibration remains dependent on device support.
