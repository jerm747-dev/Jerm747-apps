Find the feel.

Eight stride poses. A little weight through the head and shoulders. Four throw poses, four squat poses, and a momentum-carrying boost and dive. A complete jump, from the planted foot to the landing.

Run with Shift or double-tap a direction. Reverse to skid. Jump carries your running momentum; release early for a shorter hop. Flip with X, then tap X again in the air to dive. Double-tap a direction, then Speed / Shift for the blue-streak boost. Press Jump once more in the air for the upward boost; steer with the direction pad.
