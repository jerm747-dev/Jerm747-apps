ENCORE — PLATFORM DROPS & IDLE (v10)
4 November 2026

DOUBLE-TAP DOWN TO DROP
Press Down / S once to crouch. Release and press again within 0.36 seconds
to drop through a step, balcony, awning or fire escape. This works during
the squat or stand-up motion; the second tap immediately uses the falling
pose. Keyboard repeat and holding Down cannot trigger an unintended drop.
Only the starting level is ignored, so the next surface below catches him.
A held Down input will not continue dropping through more levels.
Sideways steering and the existing single airborne boost remain available.

Building rooftops in every area stay solid, as do the sidewalk, gold reward
boxes and breakable NH loot crates. Pausing, switching supports or leaving
an area cancels an unfinished double tap. Pause freezes an active fall.

IDLE THAT SETTLES IN
At the current character scale, the first stance turns the shoulders about
30 degrees toward the viewer while looking toward the route ahead.
After 2.6 seconds, the shoulders and hips open another 30 degrees. At four
seconds he glances left, then right at 5.2 seconds, returning to a frontward
stance at 6.5 seconds. At eight seconds, the original relaxed idle takes over.
Four new transparent poses share the same anatomical scale and foot line.
The existing quiet breathing movement remains. Any movement or action
interrupts the idle immediately; time airborne or paused does not advance it.

The new poses are integrated into gameplay. The existing Motion Workshop's
Idle card also follows the sequence when Animate moves is enabled.

fireball burnout, NH merchandise, optional chat and the controller layout.
