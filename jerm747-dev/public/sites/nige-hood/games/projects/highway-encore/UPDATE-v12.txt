ENCORE v12 — AFTER PARTY

On the chapter-end collection screen, choose Go to the After Party. This
optional night replay follows the same five areas. Your collected merch,
coin count and earned Gold Mic travel with you. Missed merch is still
available. Timed runs keep their separate device record.

Street level starts at Club Encore, past Low Note and Late RX. Nige’s
flashlight switches on automatically, moves with his hand and turns with
him. The opening portrait cue leaves controls active. Clubgoers and neon give
way to balcony lights and a moonlit skyline.

Walk across the fifth rooftop to meet the café host. Read the greeting, tap
Answer, then return to the collection when you’re ready.

A direct preview starts at index.html?mode=afterparty&autostart=1.
Use the collection-screen ticket to carry an existing run’s finds.
